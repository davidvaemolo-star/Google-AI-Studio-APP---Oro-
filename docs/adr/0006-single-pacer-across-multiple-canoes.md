# One Pacer sets rhythm across all canoes

The system supports up to 2 OC6 canoes (12 devices) on a single Training Controller. There is exactly one Pacer per session — designated by the coach, not assumed to be seat 1 of any specific canoe. All other 11 devices are Followers regardless of their seat number or which canoe they are in.

This means seat number alone does not uniquely identify a device; devices are identified by canoe + seat together. The firmware's current 6-device limit must be lifted to support 12 simultaneous BLE connections.
